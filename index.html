VoxelStrike

A combined-arms combat sim built on the per-column raycaster the Comanche games used, with the hot loop JIT-compiled across every core. Fly helicopters and jets, drive armour, take a warship to sea, or stand a hundred-tonne battlemech up out of a tank — over terrain you can dig holes in.

The fleet63 machines

Each class runs its own flight model, and each machine is a sidegrade rather than a rung on a ladder — speed is paid for in armour, payload or reach. The bars are scaled against the fleet’s own range, so full means “the most in the game”.

Ordnance12 round models

What a weapon puts in the air is not a table somebody maintains — it is derived from the weapon’s own fields, so an AMRAAM and a Hellfire look different because they ARE different. A lobbed rack round is a small boxy thing; a round that must fly a long way cruises on a wing; an anti-ship round is a sea-skimmer; a round that hunts radar emitters gets the long anti-radiation strakes; anything very fast is an interceptor needle; and what is left is a short fat anti-armour round. Each card lists the weapons that actually fly that shape.

Flora30 species

The world is not planted with one oak repeated. Each species is built as a base model plus procedural re-rolls, and every strip below is those rolls side by side at ONE camera distance — so the variation you can see is the variation the forest has. Which biome plants which species, and how densely, is a field on the biome entry.

Engine

The terrain is a per-column raycast over a heightmap, JIT-compiled with Numba and run across every core; an optional GPU path casts the same field per pixel, which is what lets you look straight down over a city and see buildings rather than a tilted map.

Destructible terrain
Every explosion digs a real crater into the heightmap, revealing underground strata. The carve is a fused parallel kernel, so a bomb costs about half a millisecond instead of scaling with the square of its radius.
Damage down to the voxel
A hit is recorded in the model's own frame, so the mark stays on the plate it struck while the machine yaws, banks and walks. Charred hole, molten rim, and on a battlemech the limb itself comes off.
One firing solution
The HUD lead pip, the gun convergence and the regression tests all read the same solver, so the predicted impact cannot disagree with where rounds land.
Guidance you can beat
Missiles bleed energy in a hard turn, break their seeker gimbal, lose lock behind terrain and chase flares or chaff. Symmetrically: the same rules apply to what you fire and what is fired at you.
Procedural audio
No sound files. Every engine, weapon, impact and collapse is synthesized: a voice per vehicle, a footfall per machine, and a hit that tells you whether your round went through the armour or bounced off it.
Streaming worlds
An endless world generated in worker processes running ahead of where you are flying, with settlements, forests and destructible buildings that are a pure function of position rather than of the route you took.

Correctness is gated by a headless battery of several hundred checks that runs the real session; speed is gated separately by a benchmark whose count metrics are machine-independent. Both run before anything is called done.
